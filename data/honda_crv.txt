Honda CR-V 2024 Specifications

Engine & Performance
The 2024 Honda CR-V comes with a 1.5L VTEC Turbo engine.
- Max Power: 190 PS at 6000 rpm
- Max Torque: 240 Nm at 1700-5000 rpm
- Transmission: CVT with Earth Dreams Technology

Hybrid Variant
The CR-V e:HEV features a 2.0L i-VTEC engine combined with 2 electric motors.
- System Power: 207 PS
- System Torque: 335 Nm

Dimensions
- Length: 4691 mm
- Width: 1866 mm
- Height: 1681 mm
- Wheelbase: 2701 mm

Safety
Equipped with Honda SENSING:
- CMBS (Collision Mitigation Braking System)
- LKAS (Lane Keeping Assist System)
- RDM (Road Departure Mitigation)
- ACC with LSF (Adaptive Cruise Control with Low Speed Follow)
