# Comparison: Compact SUV vs City Car

## Usage Scenarios

### Compact SUV (e.g. Honda CR-V)
Designed for families needing space and versatility.
- Pros: Spacious cabin, high ground clearance, powerful engine suitable for highway and light off-road.
- Cons: Lower fuel efficiency compared to small cars, larger dimensions make parking harder in tight spots.

### City Car / Micro EV (e.g. Wuling Air EV)
Designed for urban commuting and agility.
- Pros: Extremely agile, easy to park, very low running cost (electric), environmentally friendly.
- Cons: Limited range (requires planning for long trips), limited cargo space, max 4 passengers.

## Maintenance
- **SUV (Turbo)**: Requires regular oil changes, turbo maintenance, more moving parts.
- **EV**: Less maintenance (no oil changes), main concern is battery health and tire wear.
