Wuling Air EV Specifications

Battery & Range
- Standard Range: 17.3 kWh battery, up to 200 km range.
- Long Range: 26.7 kWh battery, up to 300 km range.
- Battery Type: Lithium Ferro-Phosphate (LFP) IP67 Rating.

Charging
- AC Charging only.
- Standard Range: ~8.5 hours (2.0 kW)
- Long Range: ~4 hours (6.6 kW)

Dimensions
- Length: 2974 mm
- Width: 1505 mm
- Height: 1631 mm
- Wheelbase: 2010 mm
- Seating Capacity: 4 persons

Features
- IoV (Internet of Vehicle) via Wuling Remote Control App
- WIND (Wuling Indonesian Command)
- 10.25" Dual Screen (Long Range)
- Easy Charging at home
